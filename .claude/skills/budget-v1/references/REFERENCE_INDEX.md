## Rules for using references

- Extract principles, not copied interfaces.
- Never reproduce third-party app names, logos, user names, or marketing claims.
- The Budget monogram and Swiss family-finance positioning are the owned direction.
- The latest canonical board overrides older iterations when they conflict.
- Actual screens must prioritize usability, accessibility, and realistic iPhone dimensions over poster aesthetics.
- Keep gradients, glow, and glass controlled enough for production performance.

## Visual review questions

- Does the screen feel like the same product as the canonical board?
- Is the main financial answer obvious within ten seconds?
- Is transparency helping hierarchy rather than reducing contrast?
- Are charts readable without glow?
- Are emojis human touches rather than decoration noise?
- Are positive, warning, and negative colors semantically correct?
- Does the screen still work at large Dynamic Type and with reduced transparency?
