# Budget V1 — Product vision

## Product thesis

Budget is not another expense tracker. It is the **financial operating dashboard for Swiss households**: one calm place to understand the month, protect future obligations, organize savings, and see the household's overall position.

The product turns fragmented financial information into three answers:

1. **Where are we now?**
2. **What is already committed or at risk?**
3. **What should we do next?**

## Primary user

A Swiss resident aged roughly 25–50 with salary income, variable bonus or commissions, several accounts, taxes to reserve, savings goals, insurance contracts, and at least one pension product. The architecture must support a couple or family even when the first release is used by one person.

## Core jobs to be done

- Know the genuinely available amount until the next income or month-end.
- Compare monthly budget with actual transactions.
- Keep savings and investments distinct from consumption.
- Reserve enough for taxes and understand arrears.
- Consolidate current, savings, broker, pension, asset, and debt positions.
- Track goals tied to real life: emergency fund, family holiday, home, vehicle, children, retirement.
- Remember annual bills, subscriptions, contracts, insurance renewal and cancellation dates.
- Import the former Notion system safely and export all data at any time.

## Brand promise

**Finances de famille. Maîtrisées.**

Supporting emotional promise: **Moins de stress, plus de vie.**

Brand character: neutral, premium, reassuring, precise, useful, human.

## V1 boundaries

V1 is local/offline first and manually managed or CSV-imported. It does not claim real-time bank connectivity, submit official tax declarations, execute investments, sell insurance, or provide regulated personalized advice.

## Product principles

- Clarity before density.
- Correctness before animation.
- Action before reporting.
- Household reality before generic fintech conventions.
- Privacy before growth shortcuts.
- Progressive disclosure before long onboarding forms.
- User ownership: data is inspectable, exportable, and deletable.

## Success criteria

A first-time user can reach a meaningful dashboard in under ten minutes using demo guidance or manual entry. A regular user can understand monthly status in under ten seconds, add a transaction in seconds, and identify at most three priority actions without reading a report.
