# Release and growth readiness

## V1 positioning

“Le tableau de bord financier des foyers suisses.”

Primary value: monthly clarity and future obligations. Secondary value: household consolidation and peace of mind.

## Trust requirements

- Explain that V1 data is local and manually entered/imported.
- Avoid “connected to all banks” until real connections exist.
- Explain tax and pension estimates and their assumptions.
- Include a methodology screen for calculations.
- Provide export and deletion before monetization pressure.

## Retention loops without manipulation

- Monthly close/review.
- Upcoming annual charges and deadlines.
- Goal milestones.
- Tax reserve health.
- Gentle uncategorized transaction cleanup.
- Household progress summary.

Do not use shame, red overload, fabricated urgency, or streak penalties around money.

## App Store package

Prepare but do not publish:

- Name/subtitle options.
- French description and keywords.
- Privacy nutrition answers grounded in actual code.
- Screenshot storyboard: welcome, available amount, budget vs actual, taxes, goal, household overview.
- Support/privacy URL placeholders.
- App icon variants based on the Budget monogram.

## Future architecture, not V1 claims

- Secure synchronization across devices/household members.
- Swiss open-banking connectivity.
- Document-assisted extraction.
- Canton-specific tax estimation engine.
- Family permissions and private/shared views.
- Optional paid tier.

Keep boundaries clean so these can be added without contaminating the offline core.
